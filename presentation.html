RUBRIC 1 - QUESTION

The question, stated precisely

For CA public high schools in 2022-2024, which school type most outperforms its expected UC freshman admit rate, after controlling for poverty, applicant GPA, and school size?

Time window

Fall terms 2022, 2023, 2024 - recent enough to be relevant, wide enough to be stable.

Population

California public high schools, Universitywide. Five school types compared.

Metric

Mean admit_rate_residual in percentage points, grouped by school_type. Precomputed by organizers.

Why this question

It surfaces schools the model underestimates - the ones "supposed" to send the fewest students to UC are quietly beating the odds.

RUBRIC 3 - RIGOR

Why the finding is trustworthy

  • Sum-then-divide. We add applicant and admit counts then divide. We never average percentages - that biases toward small schools.
  • Universitywide is not a campus sum. We report it separately, avoiding a classic aggregation error.
  • CS penalty is context, not headline. The crowded finding (CS lowers odds, Davis harshest) is shown as background so we don't duplicate what every team shows.
  • Poverty doesn't explain it. FRPM% vs residual correlation is near zero - the school-type gap holds after poverty is controlled.
  • Reproducible. Every number traces to dashboard_notebook.ipynb on the same cleaned data.
